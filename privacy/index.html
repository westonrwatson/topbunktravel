Privacy policy

What we collect

If you join the traveler waitlist, we collect your email and the answers you choose about where and when you’d like to travel. If you run a hostel, we collect your hostel’s name, city, website, a contact name, and email.

How we use it

We use this only to write to you about Top Bunk: the pass, founding member access, or a conversation about becoming a partner hostel. We don’t sell the list.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist details until you ask us to delete them, or until they’re no longer needed to launch the pass. Email us and we’ll remove you.

Contact

Questions about your information can go to topbunktravel@gmail.com.
