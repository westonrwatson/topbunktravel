How It Works

We’ve stayed in the best and worst hostels out there. The best ones made the whole trip: great people, a rooftop at sunset, staff who knew every hidden spot in town. The worst made us want to go home.So we built Top Bunk: one pass for a hand-picked network of hostels we’ve visited ourselves. You buy credits upfront, then spend them as you go. Each hostel costs 1 to 3 credits a night, depending on the city and the season.Our $150 Starter Pass gets you 10 credits, or 4 to 10 nights, to use any time within a year.
